# :material-broom: Cleaning

1. Remove the scale handle and place a large container under the dispenser.
   ![Prepare Dispenser Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
2. Disconnect the pipes from the containers and place them in a large container of hot water.
   ![Place Pipes in Hot Water Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
3. Open the **Pots** screen.
   ![Open Pots Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
4. Tap **Select** in the footer.
   ![Select Pots Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
5. Select the pots you want to clean. Use the top tab navigator to identify pots that have not been cleaned and need cleaning.
   ![Review Cleaning Status Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
6. Tap **Clean**.
   ![Start Cleaning Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
7. In the **Empty Pipe** step, choose a duration or use the default, then tap **Start**.
   ![Empty Pipe Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
8. Wait for the **Empty Pipe** step to finish.
   ![Empty Pipe Complete Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
9. Make sure the container under the dispenser is empty. Replace the hot water in the container holding the pipes with hot water and the required cleaning fluids.
   ![Prepare Cleaning Solution Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
10. Choose the dispense and soak durations, then tap **Start**.
    ![Start Soak Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
11. After the **Soak** step finishes, repeat it one more time or finish the cleaning process. Perform this step once each morning for daily cleaning, or repeat it multiple times for a weekly deep clean.
    ![Soak Complete Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
12. In the final **Empty Pipe** step, remove the container under the dispenser and replace the water in the hot-water container with hot water.
    ![Prepare Final Rinse Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
13. Choose the duration and tap **Start**.
    ![Start Final Empty Pipe Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
14. Wait for the cleaning process to finish.
    ![Cleaning Complete Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)

# :material-bottle-tonic: Recipe Ingredients

Every recipe uses a set of ingredients (materials). This page explains how to choose which ingredients a recipe uses. It is part of the **[recipe configuration](recipes.md#recipe-configuration)**.

> Ingredients themselves are created and managed on the **[Ingredients](ingredients.md)** screen. Here you only select which existing ingredients the recipe uses.

## Viewing the Recipe Ingredients

Open a recipe's details page. The **Materials** section lists the ingredients used by the recipe with their code, name, expiration and group.

*Screenshot placeholder: the recipe ingredients (materials) section.*

## Editing the Recipe Ingredients

1. Click the **edit** icon in the Materials section.
2. Use the ingredient selector to add or remove ingredients. You can search for an ingredient by name.
3. Click **Save**.

After changing the ingredients you can set their amounts per option in **[Recipe Combinations](recipe_combinations.md)**.

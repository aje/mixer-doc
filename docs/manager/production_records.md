# :material-truck-delivery: Production Records

The **Production Records** screen lists the drinks produced across your machines, so you can review what was made and investigate problems.

## Overview

Each record shows the recipe that was produced, the order information, and the outcome. Click a recipe name to quickly filter the list by that recipe.

![Production records](../assets/images/placeholder.png)

*Screenshot placeholder: the production records list.*

## Filtering Records

Use the filters at the top of the list to narrow the records:

- **Keyword** – search records by recipe.
- **Order** – filter by whether the record has an order.
- **Status** – filter by the record's status.
- **Error** – filter records that ended with an error.
- **Date range** – limit the records to a start and end date.

![Production record filters](../assets/images/placeholder.png)

*Screenshot placeholder: the filter popovers for order, status, error and date range.*

## Material Records

Open a record's **material records** to see the ingredients that were dispensed for that production, including the amounts.

The production records for one machine are also available on the machine's details page. See **[Machines](machines.md#machine-details)**. For usage charts and totals, see **[Statistics](statistics.md)**.

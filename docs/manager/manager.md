# :material-account-tie: Manager Documentation

The **Manager** area of the Mixer web dashboard is where you manage products (recipes, ingredients and customizations), organize stores and machines, monitor production, and manage user accounts.

## Overview

The manager menu is grouped into two sections:

**Product Managing**

- **[Recipes](recipes.md)** – create and manage recipes, import/export from Excel, and distribute them to stores.
- **[Ingredients](ingredients.md)** – manage the raw materials used in recipes.
- **[Customizations](customizations.md)** – manage reusable options (for example sugar level or size) that can be attached to recipes.
- **[Production Records](production_records.md)** – review the drinks produced across your machines.

**Settings**

- **[Stores](stores.md)** – organize stores into groups and assign recipes to them.
- **[Machines](machines.md)** – bind machines to stores, set machine passwords and review machine data.
- **[Users](users.md)** – manage the accounts that can sign in to the dashboard.

## Typical Workflow

A common end‑to‑end flow for a manager is:

1. Create the **[ingredients](ingredients.md)** and **[customizations](customizations.md)** you need.
2. Create your **[recipes](recipes.md)** and configure their ingredients, customizations and combinations.
3. **[Distribute the recipes](recipes.md#distributing-recipes-to-stores)** to the relevant stores or store groups.
4. Organize your **[stores](stores.md)** and **[bind machines](machines.md)** to them.
5. Monitor **[production records](production_records.md)** and **[statistics](statistics.md)**.

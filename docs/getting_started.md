# :material-rocket-launch: Getting Started

Follow these steps to prepare the pots and dispense a recipe.

## Bind an Ingredient

1. Open the **Pots** screen.
   ![Open Pots Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
2. Tap a pot with no ingredient assigned.
   ![Select Empty Pot Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
3. Select an ingredient from the available materials list.
   ![Select Ingredient Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
4. Confirm that the ingredient name appears on the pot.
   ![Bound Ingredient Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)

## Fill and Initiate a Pot

1. Tap the **Menu icon (three vertical dots)** on the pot.
   ![Open Pot Menu Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
2. Select **Refill**.
   ![Select Refill Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
3. Adjust the slider to the new total volume.
   ![Adjust Volume Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
4. Tap **Top Up**.
   ![Top Up Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
5. Wait for the brief initialization to finish, if it starts.
   ![Initialization Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)

## Dispense a Recipe

1. Open **Recipes**.
   ![Open Recipes Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
2. Select a recipe.
   ![Select Recipe Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
3. Select the specifications.
   ![Select Specifications Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
4. Check that the ingredients are available and the dispensers are calibrated.
   ![Check Availability Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
5. Place a cup under the dispenser.
   ![Place Cup Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
6. Tap **Dispense**.
   ![Dispense Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
7. Wait for the dispensing process to finish.
   ![Dispensing Progress Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
8. Check the completion summary.
   ![Completion Summary Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)

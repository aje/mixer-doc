# :material-scale-balance: Manual Dispensing

You can manually dispense a specific amount of an ingredient.

1. Open the **Pots** screen.
   ![Open Pots Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
2. Tap the pot you want to dispense. The pot details open in the sidebar.
   ![Open Pot Details Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
3. Select or enter the desired amount in grams (g).
   ![Set Dispense Amount Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
4. Place a cup under the dispenser.
   ![Place Cup Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
5. Tap **Dispense**.
   ![Start Manual Dispensing Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
6. Monitor the real-time dispensing weight and the final accuracy summary.
   ![Dispensing Summary Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
7. Tap **Stop** if you need to interrupt the process.
   ![Stop Dispensing Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)

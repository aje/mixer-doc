# :material-scale-balance: Manual Dispensing

You can manually dispense a specific amount of an ingredient.

1. Open the **Pots** screen. 
2. Tap the pot you want to dispense. The pot details open in the sidebar.
   ![Open Pot Details Placeholder](assets/images/cn/pots/pots-item.png)
3. Select or enter the desired amount in grams (g).
   ![Set Dispense Amount Placeholder](assets/images/cn/pots/manual-dispense.png)
4. Place a cup under the dispenser. 
5. Tap **Dispense**. 
6. Monitor the real-time dispensing weight and the final accuracy summary.
   ![Monitor the real-time dispensing](assets/images/cn/pots/manual-loading.png)
7. Tap **Stop** if you need to interrupt the process.

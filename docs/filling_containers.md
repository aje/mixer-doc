# :material-water-plus: Filling and Initiating Containers

1. Refill the pot with the required fluid.
   ![Refill Pot Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
2. Place a large cup under the dispenser.
   ![Place Large Cup Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
3. Open the **Pots** screen.
   ![Open Pots Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
4. Tap the **Menu icon (three vertical dots)** on the pot.
   ![Open Pot Menu Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
5. Select **Refill**.
   ![Select Refill Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
6. Adjust the slider to the new total volume.
   ![Adjust Volume Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
7. Tap **Top Up**.
   ![Top Up Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
8. Wait for the brief initialization to finish, if it starts.
   ![Initialization Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)

## Refilling Multiple Pots

1. Refill the pots with the required fluids.
   ![Refill Pots Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
2. Place a large cup under the dispenser.
   ![Place Large Cup Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
3. Open the **Pots** screen.
   ![Open Pots Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
4. Tap **Select** in the footer.
   ![Select Pots Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
5. Select the pots you refilled.
   ![Select Refilled Pots Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
6. Tap **Refill**.
   ![Refill Selected Pots Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
7. Wait for the pots to initiate one by one.
   ![Pot Initialization Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)
8. Confirm the process.
   ![Confirm Refill Placeholder](assets/images/placeholder.png)

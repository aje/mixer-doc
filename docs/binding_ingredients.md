# :material-link-variant: Binding and Unbinding Ingredients

1. Open the **Pots** screen.
2. Tap a pot with no ingredient assigned.
   ![Open Pots Placeholder](assets/images/cn/pots/pots.png)
3. Select an ingredient from the available materials list.
   ![Select Ingredient Placeholder](assets/images/cn/pots/bind.png)
4. Confirm that the ingredient name appears on the pot. 

## Unbinding an Ingredient

1. Open the **Pots** screen.
2. Tap the **Menu icon (three vertical dots)** next to the pot.
   ![Open Pots Placeholder](assets/images/cn/pots/pot-menu.png)
3. Select **Unbind Pot**. 

       ![Open Pot Menu Placeholder](assets/images/cn/pots/pot-menu-unbind.png)

4. Confirm the action.
   ![Confirm the action](assets/images/cn/pots/pot-unbind-confirm.png)
